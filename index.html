Manifesto

This isn't fashion. It's armor with a wink.

Bunny Anarchy was born from the exhaustion of being called "feminist" like it was an insult. It refuses to shrink, apologize, or play by rules written by men. It isn't anti-everything — it's anti-sexism. It makes feminism bold, loud, and yes: hot.

Every piece is a contradiction: soft and brutal, playful and prepared, cute but dangerous. The brand moves like organized anarchy — no leaders, no lore, just vibe. It isn't rebellion for rebellion's sake. It's survival wrapped in pink, a refusal to play someone else's game.

Chill rage. Glitter in the wound. Post-patriarchy glow-up.

I love you Banksy. Call me. ♥
